What's actually happening: each camera runs its own independent tracker with its own local ID space. Camera A's "person #4" means nothing to Camera B. Aperture's re-ID engine compares an HSV color-histogram descriptor of each person's appearance across cameras, inside a short time window, and accepts a match once the similarity clears a threshold. This demo runs on a synthetic two-camera corridor so it's fully self-contained; point vision_pipeline.py at two real video files (YOLOv8 + ByteTrack under the hood) to run the identical matching logic on real footage. See README.md for the honest limitations of color-histogram matching versus a learned re-ID embedding, and what a v2 would add.
